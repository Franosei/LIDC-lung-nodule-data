# Figure 1. Label-assignment and case-exclusion effects

(A) Paired AUC differences and (B) paired Brier-score differences for the fixed combined-logistic predictions. Label assignment compares the two full-cohort median rules on identical nodules (median-3-malignant minus median-3-benign). Case exclusion compares the restricted population with the full population while retaining the stated label rule. Intervals use patient-level bootstrap resampling. Vermillion circles indicate label assignment; blue squares and green diamonds indicate exclusion under the median-3-benign and median-3-malignant rules, respectively.
