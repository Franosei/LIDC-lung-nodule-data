# Figure 1. Reference-label misclassification changes apparent clinical AI performance

(A) Inter-reader agreement and disagreement. (B) AUC when unchanged predictions are scored against six reference definitions. (C) Same-reader AUC inflation. (D) Frozen-model performance as locally indeterminate nodules are progressively relabelled malignant; the dashed line is the panel-implied diameter boundary. (E) Combined logistic-regression AUC after adding lower-reader-count nodules with single-reader labels. (F) Rating profiles of the BIC-selected exchangeable-rater latent classes. (G) AUC on the diagnosis-linked subset against radiologist-derived references and confirmed diagnosis. Points are estimates and horizontal intervals are patient-bootstrap 95% confidence intervals where shown.
